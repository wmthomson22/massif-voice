- what would change our mind: if the first mobile phone customer order (announced late Dec, units/pricing not disclosed) doesn't convert to volume shipments by year end we have to ask whether the problem is the technology or the customer. either answer means the position gets cut
- we are not adding here. we're not selling either. position is ~5% now from 8% at peak
- tape is thin, stale fundamentals, retail-heavy shareholder base, so the stock trades on sentiment not progress. that cuts both ways
